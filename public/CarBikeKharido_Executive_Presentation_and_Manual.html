Operational Guidelines

Comprehensive User & Operator Manual

Step-by-step instructions designed for buyers, sellers, and system administrators to maximize platform utility.

1

Discovering Your Ideal Vehicle

Navigate to the primary search bar or use the top category selectors (Cars, Bikes, EVs). Enter model names, brands, or body formats (e.g., “Tata Punch”, “Classic 350”, “SUV”). The multi-token engine will instantly match relevant records.

2

Inspecting Specifications & On-Road Price

Click on any vehicle card to open its comprehensive detail page. Select your location to view the exact on-road price breakdown (Ex-showroom, RTO registration, insurance). Use the interactive color swatch and 360-degree studio view to inspect the exterior.

3

Using Rider Fit & Running Cost Calculators

For two-wheelers, navigate to Rider Fit to input your height and inseam. The system calculates foot reach and comfort bands. Use Running Cost to project 5-year fuel, servicing, and tyre costs against electric alternatives.

4

Connecting with Verified Sellers & Dealers

Click “Unlock Contact Details” to reveal the direct phone number and WhatsApp link of the vehicle owner or authorized showroom, ensuring zero middleman commissions.

1

Initiating Vehicle Listing

Click “Sell” on the navigation bar. Select vehicle type (Car or Bike), manufacturer, model year, variant, and odometer reading. The platform will automatically pull factory specifications and price benchmarks.

2

Uploading High-Resolution Photography

Upload clear photographs covering front three-quarter, rear, interior cockpit, and tyre condition. The automated image processing pipeline ensures crisp rendering while stripping sensitive geographical metadata.

3

Managing Buyer Enquiries Securely

Incoming buyer requests are routed directly to your verified contact number. Your exact residential location remains private, showing only your locality and city to prospective buyers.

1

Dealership Subscription & Lead Credit Quotas

Dealerships manage their subscription tier (Silver, Gold, Platinum). Lead credits are automatically deducted when unlocking high-intent buyer profiles, with real-time audit logs to eliminate duplicate deductions.

2

Listing Moderation & Quality Control

Administrators review submitted listings for price accuracy, title authenticity, and image clarity before awarding “Verified Listing” badges.

3

Promotions & Featured Showcases

Highlight high-priority dealership stock in the “Trending Vehicles” and “Cinematic Showroom Carousel” to maximize organic buyer engagement.
