1. What CarBikeKharido is

CarBikeKharido is an India-focused marketplace for cars and two-wheelers in one place. Visitors can browse new models, explore used listings, sell a vehicle, compare options, and use decision tools — without being forced to create an account first.

The simple promise

Browse freely. Decide with better numbers. Buy or sell with clear next steps — and only sign in when you need to contact someone or publish a listing.

The home screen opens with a full-width brand experience: search for cars or bikes, switch between new and used, jump into popular models, and reach the tools that competitors rarely put above the fold — Rider Fit, Running Cost, and EMI.

2. Who it is for

New-vehicle shoppers

People researching cars or bikes, comparing variants and prices, estimating EMI, and shortlisting models before visiting a showroom.

Used-vehicle buyers

People filtering listings by city, price, year and mileage, then contacting private sellers or dealers after a quick phone check.

Sellers

Owners who want a guided listing flow — details, photos, phone verification — and a published ad or a saved draft.

Two-wheeler riders

Riders who need more than a brochure: which bike fits their height and weight, and what it really costs to own per kilometre.

4. Workflow: Finding a new vehicle

Home / Search → Filters & brands → Model page → Price · Variants · Tools → Shortlist / EMI

Step by step

  1. Start on the home screen. Choose New, then Cars or Bikes. Type a model name or use quick picks (for cars: Electric, Maruti, Tata, Hyundai; for bikes: Scooters, Royal Enfield, Honda, TVS).
  2. Browse the catalogue. Search shows India-wide models. Narrow by category (cars, EVs, bikes, scooters), brand, and name. Sort by name or price.
  3. Open a model page. You see the overview photo, on-road price range, mileage highlights, and how many variants exist.
  4. Use the section tabs to jump to Price, Variants, Specs, Compare with rivals, Offers, and More tools — without losing your place.
  5. Decide with tools. From the model page you can open EMI, Running Cost (for bikes), or the full Compare tool. Heart a model to keep it in your wishlist.

What “done” looks like for this journey

The visitor leaves with a shortlist, a clear price band, an EMI estimate, and (for bikes) a sense of fit and running cost — ready for a showroom visit. Online booking and payment for a brand-new vehicle are not part of the product yet.

5. Workflow: Buying a used vehicle

Used marketplace → Filters → Listing detail → Sign in → Contact seller

Step by step

  1. Open Used from the home search toggles or go to the used marketplace area. Featured listings and quick filters help you start fast.
  2. Refine the search. Apply filters such as vehicle type, price, city, fuel, year, and mileage until the list feels right.
  3. Open a listing. View the photo gallery, key specs, seller comments, and similar vehicles nearby.
  4. Contact the seller. Call, WhatsApp, or email become available after a short phone verification — so casual browsing stays open, but spam stays down.
  5. Save favourites. Heart listings and return later; signed-in visitors can use price alerts on the marketplace wishlist.

Also available

Share a listing link. Report a suspicious ad. Compare several used listings side by side with a visual chart and an option to print or save as PDF.

Aggregated listings

When multi-source inventory is connected, a unified browse view can show used vehicles collected from more than one place — still with filters and cards.

6. Workflow: Selling your vehicle

Details → Photos → Seller & verify → Review → Publish or draft
  1. Step 1 — Details: Vehicle type, brand, model, year, fuel, mileage, price, city, and description.
  2. Step 2 — Media: Upload photos (and video where allowed). Progress saves as you go so a half-finished ad is not lost.
  3. Step 3 — You: Seller name and phone. Confirm the number with a one-time code before publishing.
  4. Step 4 — Review: Check everything, then publish live or keep a draft.
  5. Success: Confirmation screen with handy share links for WhatsApp and social posts.

Support tip

If a seller says they never received a verification code, phone messaging may not be connected in that environment yet. The listing form itself is complete; delivery of SMS depends on the messaging service being switched on.

8. Smart tools & calculators

These tools are the heart of CarBikeKharido’s product personality.

EMI Calculator

Drag sliders for vehicle price, down payment, tenure, and interest rate. Instantly see monthly EMI, total interest, and total amount payable. Also available as a teaser on the home page. Useful before talking to a bank or dealer finance desk — it is an estimate, not a sanctioned loan quote.

Rider Fit Bike-first

Enter height, weight, and how you like to ride (commute, tour, sport, and so on). The tool ranks bikes by how well they match your body and intent — seat reach, manageability, and style fit — not only by popularity.

Why this matters

Most portals answer “what’s popular?” Rider Fit answers “what will feel right under me?” That is a conversation dealers rarely start with data.

Running Cost (ownership cost) Bike-first

Pick one or more bikes and set how far you ride each year and how long you plan to keep the vehicle. The calculator breaks down fuel or electricity, service, consumables, insurance, and expected resale — then shows total cost of ownership and cost per kilometre.

Ideal for the classic debate: “Is the cheaper scooter actually cheaper after three years?”

10. News, language & everyday helpers

Language

The product is bilingual. Switching between English and हिंदी updates navigation, buttons, and core labels so Hindi-first users are not stuck in English-only UI.

News & guides

The News area hosts buying guides and article pages. Treat current articles as illustrative content while the editorial programme scales — structure and reading experience are in place; the library will grow.

Wishlist

Heart icons let shoppers save models or used listings. Catalogue favourites stay on the device; marketplace wishlist and price alerts are for signed-in visitors.

Dealer strip (home)

The home page shows example authorised dealers for inspiration. Live city-wide dealer maps and one-tap calling are on the roadmap; today this block should be described as a preview, not a complete locator product.

12. What is still growing

A good manual tells the truth. These areas have screens or promises that are not yet complete end-to-end for every visitor:

  • Phone OTP by SMS — the verification screens exist; SMS delivery depends on the messaging partner being live.
  • City-wise on-road prices — price bands are shown; changing city does not yet recalculate every figure.
  • Dealer locator — sample dealers only; call / directions / test-drive booking are not live.
  • Offers & sponsored CTAs — may display campaigns; “Claim” does not always open a real lead form yet.
  • User reviews & 360° views — tabs are present on purpose; content is marked coming soon.
  • News library — layout ready; many articles are still placeholder-quality.
  • Trust promises (history report, price-lock) — homepage messaging ahead of productised tools.
  • Company pages (About, Careers, Contact, Privacy, Terms) — linked in the footer but not filled yet.
  • Online purchase of a new vehicle — research and contact paths exist; checkout does not.

How to talk about the product today

“Browse India’s cars and bikes, use Rider Fit and Running Cost to decide smarter, and buy or sell used with a clear marketplace flow.” Avoid promising live SMS, live dealer CRM, or full editorial depth until those journeys are finished.

13. Quick answers

Do I need an account to look around?

No. Browse, search, EMI, Rider Fit, Running Cost, and new-model pages work without signing in.

When do I need to verify my phone?

When contacting a used-vehicle seller, publishing a listing, or using marketplace alerts that need identity.

Is CarBikeKharido only for bikes?

No. Cars and bikes share the brand. Some unique tools (Rider Fit, Running Cost) are two-wheeler-specific by design.

Can I buy a car online end-to-end?

Not yet. The product helps you research and connect; showroom purchase and finance still happen offline or through partners to come.

What should support say when someone asks “Why is my OTP not arriving?”

Acknowledge the step, explain that SMS may not be enabled in this environment yet, and escalate to the team that manages messaging — do not invent a code for the customer.

Where should partners start?

New OEMs/dealers: model pages + offers + EMI. Used dealers: marketplace listings + compare. Two-wheeler brands: Rider Fit and Running Cost as the conversation openers.
